Exoskeleton, Structural

Hip Module Design & Fabrication

MAY 2026 → PRESENT

Led design and manufacture of the hip module for the Exoskeleton platform, from Fusion 360 CAD through FEA validation, 3D printing, CNC machining, and final assembly, iterating through several real structural failures along the way.

CAD / Analysis
Fusion 360, FEA
Materials
PLA → Onyx → CF
Processes
FDM, CF print, CNC
  • Diagnosed a fractured PLA hip cuff and cracked cuff slider, tracing the failure to a high-stress zone at the strut interface. Those findings directly shaped the reinforced carbon fiber redesign.
  • Redesigned the hip abductor from scratch after the PLA prototype, validating the new geometry with FEA before committing to final material.
  • Milled and hand-finished carbon fiber struts in the machine shop, working through drilling-speed failures (one strut cracked) and post-processing exposed fiber for safe wear.
  • Caught and corrected a 200-micron shoulder screw bore tolerance issue caused by anodizing buildup on outsourced aluminum parts before it reached final assembly.
  • Designed custom motor mounts for both AK60-6 and AK80 actuators to validate fit against the strut assembly on a mechanical breadboard.
  • Machined and anodized the abduction bracket in 6061-T6 aluminum, the finished version of the part after resolving tolerance and hole-spacing issues found in earlier iterations.

Bowden Cable Transmission: Ankle Actuation

MAY 2026 → PRESENT

Prototyped the Bowden cable transmission that lets the ankle's drive motor sit proximally on the leg instead of at the joint itself, directly cutting the distal mass a wearer has to swing with every step.

Function
Remote actuation
Insert material
TPU (flexible)
Variants tested
Tapered / Straight
Guide
Dual carbon fiber tube
  • Designed, printed, and compared two TPU cable insert geometries (tapered and straight) to evaluate cable routing smoothness and fit.
  • Assembled the full carriage: sliding rail, motor mount plate, and dual-tube guide block, iterating through early print failures to improve reliability.
  • Working with a graduate student on the broader transmission system, contributing prototype design and hands-on assembly.

Diaphragm material

Diagnostics
