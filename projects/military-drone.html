Military Drone Concept: Multi-Payload Quadcopter

CLASS FINAL PROJECT

Designed a fully modeled (non-functional) quadcopter concept in SolidWorks as a team final project, featuring a deployable landing leg mechanism, a 2-axis camera gimbal, and mounted payload modules including a flashlight, flare, and laser pointer.

Software
SolidWorks
Type
Design Concept (Non-Functional)
Team
Group Project
  • Modeled a full quadcopter assembly including body shell, four frame arms, motors, propellers, and a hinged multi-leg landing gear system.
  • Helped design a 2-axis camera gimbal assembly (yaw-to-roll and pitch-to-roll elbow joints) to stabilize a mounted camera module, as part of a 4-person team.
  • Integrated deployable payload modules (a flashlight, flare, and laser pointer), each mounted via custom clamp holders on the frame.
