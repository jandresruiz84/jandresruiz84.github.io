Exoskeleton, Full Assembly

Full Mechanical Assembly

JUNE 2026 → PRESENT

Assembled the complete mechanical chain for the lower-limb exoskeleton leg, combining the hip bracket, thigh link, and ankle module built in earlier iterations into one wearable structure.

Status
Mechanical complete
Next step
Motor electronics
Role
Assembly / Fit check
  • Assembled the full mechanical chain from the hip bracket through the carbon fiber thigh link to the ankle module, combining parts fabricated across earlier iterations.
  • Fit-checked the assembly on body, adjusting strap tension and hip alignment for comfortable wear and clearance through a walking range of motion.
  • Next step is wiring and integrating the drive motor electronics to bring the assembly from a mechanical mockup to a powered system.
