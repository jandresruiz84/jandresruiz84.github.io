Bowden Cable Transmission: Ankle Actuation

MAY 2026 → PRESENT

Prototyped the Bowden cable transmission that moves the 485 g ankle actuator off the distal leg to sit proximally instead, directly cutting the distal mass a wearer has to swing with every step.

Function
Remote actuation
Insert material
TPU (flexible)
Variants tested
Tapered / Straight
Guide
Dual carbon fiber tube
  • Designed, printed, and tested two TPU cable insert geometries (tapered and straight), selecting the tapered design for better cable routing and fit.
  • Assembled the full carriage: sliding rail, motor mount plate, and dual-tube guide block, iterating through early print failures to improve reliability.
  • Working with graduate researchers on the broader transmission system, contributing prototype design and hands-on assembly.
