Shoulder Mount L-Bracket Redesign

NOVEMBER 2026

Redesigned the shoulder mounting bracket for the lab's robotic arm after tap tests showed the original ringing at about 2.6 Hz for more than 4 seconds after each impact, corrupting accelerometer data.

Old resonance
~2.6 Hz
New resonance
~4.5 Hz
Settling time
4s → ~1s
Validation
6 taps × 3 poses
  • Diagnosed the original bracket's low-frequency ringing (about 2.6 Hz, settling over 4 seconds after each tap) as a source of corrupted accelerometer data on the arm.
  • Iterated the bracket through 3 design revisions to stiffen the load path between the shoulder actuator and the first link.
  • Validated the redesign with instrumented tap tests, 6 taps per bracket across 3 arm poses (circle-centre, flexed, extended), raising the dominant resonance from about 2.6 Hz to about 4.5 Hz and cutting vibration settling time from over 4 seconds to roughly 1 second, a better than 3x improvement.
