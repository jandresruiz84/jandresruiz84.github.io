Bota Force/Torque Sensor Tube Clamp

NOVEMBER 2026

Designed a 3D-printed split clamp that mounts a Bota Medusa 6-axis force/torque sensor and its output handle onto the wrist exoskeleton's 32 mm HEBI tube. After a first version passed FEA, I redesigned the top half into a lower, solid cone that is stiffer, easier to assemble, and bolts onto the existing TRT-500 clamp base without reprinting it.

Peak von Mises
2.91 → 1.88 MPa
Max deflection
0.021 → 0.008 mm
Safety factor (PET)
~19 → ~29
Clamp height
34 → 28 mm
  • Designed the clamp in Fusion around the sensor's real interface: a seat for the Medusa base, an 8-hole M3 bolt circle, and a locating pin, with the bolt pattern rotated so all 8 screw channels clear the clamp's heat-set inserts and can be driven from underneath.
  • Kept the split line, 32.3 mm bore, 40 mm clamp width, and insert spacing identical to the earlier TRT-500 sensor clamp, so the new top half bolts onto the original, already-printed bottom half. I verified this with interference and hole-alignment checks in a full sensor, handle, and tube assembly.
  • Ran static stress studies in Fusion Simulation with a 75 N side load applied at the top of the handle (about 145 to 151 mm above the tube), the bottom half fixed, and PET material properties. V1 showed a 2.91 MPa peak, concentrated where the pedestal necked into the clamp.
  • Redesigned the top as a solid cone that blends from the 40 mm clamp into the 56 mm sensor plate, and lowered it from 34 to 28 mm. That shortened the lever arm and removed the neck: peak stress dropped 35% to 1.88 MPa and deflection dropped 60% to 0.008 mm, a safety factor of about 29 against PET yield.
  • Worked out the system limits beyond the part itself: at a 12 N·m sensor overload rating the sensor tops out near 60 N at the handle, well below the clamp's estimated printed-part failure load of several hundred newtons, so the sensor, not the bracket, sets the usable range.
  • Designed for printing and assembly: no supports needed above the bore, minimal visible holes, and a standard hardware list (8× M3×8 sensor screws, 4× M3×12 clamp screws into heat-set inserts). Printed in PETG with 5 walls and 40% gyroid infill.
